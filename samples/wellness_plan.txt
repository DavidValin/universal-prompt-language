Create a [[[WEEKS]]]-week health and wellness plan for a [[[AGE]]]-year-old person whose primary goal is "[[[PRIMARY_GOAL]]]".

Current fitness level: [[[CURRENT_FITNESS_LEVEL]]]
Available for exercise: [[[AVAILABLE_DAYS_PER_WEEK]]] days/week, [[[MINUTES_PER_SESSION]]] minutes/session.
Current sleep: [[[SLEEP_HOURS_CURRENT]]] hrs/night → target: [[[SLEEP_HOURS_TARGET]]] hrs/night.
Stress level: [[[STRESS_LEVEL]]].

Available equipment:
{{{for ITEM in EQUIPMENT}}}
- [[[ITEM]]]
{{{end for}}}

Dietary preferences/restrictions:
{{{for PREF in DIETARY_PREFERENCES}}}
- [[[PREF]]]
{{{end for}}}

Structure the plan as follows:

1. **Goal assessment** — restate the primary goal ("[[[PRIMARY_GOAL]]]") and define 2-3 measurable success metrics (e.g. weight, reps, sleep quality score).

2. **Fitness plan** — create a weekly workout schedule for [[[AVAILABLE_DAYS_PER_WEEK]]] days, each session [[[MINUTES_PER_SESSION]]] minutes long. For each day:
   - Day of the week.
   - Workout type (strength, cardio, mobility, rest/recovery).
   - 3-5 specific exercises with sets and reps, using only the available equipment.

{{{if INCLUDE_MEAL_PLAN}}}
3. **Nutrition guidance** — provide a sample day meal plan (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack) that respects the dietary preferences. For each meal:
   - Meal name and a short description.
   - Approximate calories and protein.
   Then list 3 general nutrition principles to follow throughout the plan.
{{{end if}}}

4. **Sleep improvement** — provide 4-5 actionable sleep hygiene steps to move from [[[SLEEP_HOURS_CURRENT]]] to [[[SLEEP_HOURS_TARGET]]] hours per night. Calibrate the steps to a [[[STRESS_LEVEL]]] stress level.

5. **Stress management** — recommend 3 stress-reduction techniques appropriate for someone with a [[[STRESS_LEVEL]]] stress level. For each:
   - Technique name.
   - How to practice it (2-3 sentences).
   - Recommended frequency.

{{{if INCLUDE_HABIT_TRACKER}}}
6. **Weekly habit tracker** — create a checklist of 5-7 daily habits to track across the [[[WEEKS]]] weeks. Present it as a table header with the habits and 7 day columns.
{{{end if}}}

7. **Progression plan** — describe how to progressively increase the difficulty of the fitness plan every 2 weeks across the [[[WEEKS]]]-week period.

End with a one-paragraph motivational message that ties the fitness, nutrition, sleep, and stress components together.

Disclaimer: This plan is informational and not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a healthcare provider before starting a new exercise or nutrition program.
