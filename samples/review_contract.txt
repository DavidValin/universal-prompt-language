--
name: review_contract
title: Review a Legal Contract
desc: Review a contract clause-by-clause, flagging risks, missing provisions, and ambiguous language, with severity ratings and suggested revisions.
params:
  contract_text:
    type: long_string
    desc: The full text of the contract to review
    def: ""
  contract_type:
    type: option_single
    desc: The type of contract
    opts:
      - "nda"
      - "employment"
      - "saas"
      - "consulting"
      - "vendor"
      - "lease"
      - "general"
    def: "general"
  reviewer_role:
    type: option_single
    desc: The perspective of the reviewer
    opts:
      - "business_owner"
      - "individual"
      - "in_house_counsel"
      - "startup_founder"
    def: "business_owner"
  governing_law:
    type: string
    desc: The governing law jurisdiction (e.g. "California, USA")
    def: ""
  focus_areas:
    type: option_multi
    desc: Specific areas to focus the review on
    etype: string
    opts:
      - "liability"
      - "payment_terms"
      - "termination"
      - "ip_ownership"
      - "confidentiality"
      - "indemnification"
      - "data_protection"
    def:
      - "liability"
      - "payment_terms"
      - "termination"
  include_plain_summary:
    type: boolean
    desc: Whether to include a plain-language summary of the contract
    def: true
  include_redlines:
    type: boolean
    desc: Whether to suggest specific revised wording for flagged clauses
    def: true
  max_flags:
    type: number
    desc: Maximum number of issues to flag
    def: 15
--
Review the following contract from the perspective of a [[[REVIEWER_ROLE]]].

Contract type: [[[CONTRACT_TYPE]]]
{{{if GOVERNING_LAW}}}
Governing law: [[[GOVERNING_LAW]]]
{{{end if}}}

Contract text:
"""
[[[CONTRACT_TEXT]]]
"""

Focus the review on these areas:
{{{for AREA in FOCUS_AREAS}}}
- [[[AREA]]]
{{{end for}}}

Review instructions:

1. Read the entire contract carefully. Identify clauses relevant to each focus area.

2. Flag up to [[[MAX_FLAGS]]] issues. For each issue:
   - **Clause** — quote the relevant clause or a short excerpt.
   - **Category** — which focus area it falls under.
   - **Severity** — rate as "high", "medium", or "low".
   - **Problem** — explain the risk or ambiguity in 1-2 sentences.
   {{{if INCLUDE_REDLINES}}}
   - **Suggested revision** — provide specific revised wording that better protects a [[[REVIEWER_ROLE]]].
   {{{end if}}}

3. Identify any **missing provisions** that a contract of type "[[[CONTRACT_TYPE]]]" should typically contain but are absent from this text.

{{{if INCLUDE_PLAIN_SUMMARY}}}
4. **Plain-language summary** — summarize the contract in 3-5 sentences that a non-lawyer [[[REVIEWER_ROLE]]] can understand. Cover: what each party agrees to do, what happens on a breach, and how the contract ends.
{{{end if}}}

5. **Overall risk assessment** — give the contract a risk level (low / moderate / high) and list the top 3 actions the reviewer should take before signing.

Disclaimer: This review is informational and does not constitute legal advice. Consult a licensed attorney in {{{if GOVERNING_LAW}}}[[[GOVERNING_LAW]]]{{{end if}}}{{{if !GOVERNING_LAW}}}the relevant jurisdiction{{{end if}}} before signing.
