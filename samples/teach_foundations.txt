--

params:
  subject:
    type: string
    desc: The subject to teach the foundations of
    def: "calculus"
  level:
    type: option_single
    desc: Education level of the learner
    opts:
      - "elementary"
      - "middle_school"
      - "high_school"
      - "undergraduate"
      - "graduate"
    def: "high_school"
  format:
    type: option_single
    desc: Delivery format of the lesson
    opts:
      - "lecture"
      - "interactive"
      - "slides"
      - "notes"
    def: "notes"
  duration_minutes:
    type: number
    desc: Total lesson duration in minutes
    def: 45
  include_exercises:
    type: boolean
    desc: Whether to include practice exercises
    def: true
  include_summary:
    type: boolean
    desc: Whether to include a recap at the end
    def: true
  learning_goals:
    type: list
    desc: Specific learning goals the lesson must achieve
    etype: string
    def:
      - "Define the core vocabulary of the subject"
      - "Explain why the subject exists and what problem it solves"
  materials:
    type: object
    desc: Materials the learner has available
    ofields:
      has_calculator:
        type: boolean
        def: true
      has_internet:
        type: boolean
        def: true
      preferred_language:
        type: string
        desc: Natural language for the lesson
        def: "English"

Role: You are a patient curriculum designer who builds foundational lessons that scale to the learner.

Design a foundational lesson on "[[[SUBJECT]]]" for a [[[LEVEL]]] learner.

Delivery format: [[[FORMAT]]].
Total time budget: [[[DURATION_MINUTES]]] minutes.
Language: [[[MATERIALS.PREFERRED_LANGUAGE]]].

The lesson MUST achieve these learning goals, in order:
{{{for GOAL in LEARNING_GOALS}}}
- [[[GOAL]]]
{{{end for}}}

Calibrate the depth and vocabulary to a [[[LEVEL]]] learner:
{{{LEVEL = "elementary" ? "Use simple words, short sentences, and everyday analogies. Avoid jargon." : ""}}}{{{LEVEL = "middle_school" ? "Introduce key terms but define each one the first time it appears." : ""}}}{{{LEVEL = "high_school" ? "Use proper terminology and show worked examples step by step." : ""}}}{{{LEVEL = "undergraduate" ? "Use formal definitions, notation, and cite the assumptions behind each step." : ""}}}{{{LEVEL = "graduate" ? "Assume mathematical maturity; focus on generality, edge cases, and connections to other fields." : ""}}}

Structure the lesson as:
1. Hook (2-3 sentences): why [[[SUBJECT]]] is worth learning for a [[[LEVEL]]] learner.
2. Core ideas: build up the foundations one concept at a time, each followed by a one-line check for understanding.

{{{if FORMAT = "interactive"}}}
After each concept, include a question you would ask the learner to check understanding, plus the answer you expect.
{{{end if}}}
{{{if FORMAT = "slides"}}}
Split the lesson into slide-sized chunks and give each slide a title.
{{{end if}}}

{{{if MATERIALS.HAS_CALCULATOR}}}
Note: the learner has a calculator available, so exercises may require numeric computation.
{{{end if}}}
{{{if !MATERIALS.HAS_INTERNET}}}
Do not require the learner to look anything up online; make the lesson self-contained.
{{{end if}}}

{{{if INCLUDE_EXERCISES}}}
Include 3 practice exercises that increase in difficulty. For each:
- The exercise statement.
- A hint (one sentence).
- The worked solution.
{{{end if}}}

{{{if INCLUDE_SUMMARY}}}
End with a 5-bullet recap titled "What you should remember" and a single transfer question that asks the learner to apply [[[SUBJECT]]] to a new situation.
{{{end if}}}

Keep the whole lesson deliverable within [[[DURATION_MINUTES]]] minutes.
--
