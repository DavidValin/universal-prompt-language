Analyze the following personal finance data and produce a strategy to improve financial health.

Monthly income sources:
{{{for INCOME in MONTHLY_INCOME}}}
- [[[INCOME.SOURCE]]]: [[[CURRENCY]]] [[[INCOME.AMOUNT]]] ([[[INCOME.FREQUENCY]]])
{{{end for}}}

Monthly expenses:
{{{for EXPENSE in MONTHLY_EXPENSES}}}
- [[[EXPENSE.NAME]]]: [[[CURRENCY]]] [[[EXPENSE.AMOUNT]]] | category: [[[EXPENSE.CATEGORY]]] | fixed: [[[EXPENSE.IS_FIXED]]]{{{if EXPENSE.DESCRIPTION}}} | note: [[[EXPENSE.DESCRIPTION]]]{{{end if}}}
{{{end for}}}

Target savings rate: [[[SAVINGS_TARGET_PCT]]]% of net income.
Risk tolerance: [[[RISK_TOLERANCE]]].

Financial goals:
{{{for GOAL in GOALS}}}
- [[[GOAL]]]
{{{end for}}}

Structure the strategy as follows:

1. **Cash flow snapshot** — calculate and present:
   - Total monthly income (normalize biweekly/weekly/irregular income to a monthly equivalent).
   - Total monthly expenses (split into fixed and variable).
   - Net monthly cash flow (income minus expenses).
   - Current effective savings rate (savings as a percentage of net income, or 0 if nothing is allocated to savings).

2. **Health assessment** — rate the overall financial health as "strong", "stable", or "at-risk" based on the cash flow snapshot and the gap between the current and target savings rates. Justify the rating in 2-3 sentences.

{{{if INCLUDE_CATEGORY_BREAKDOWN}}}
3. **Category breakdown** — group expenses by category and present:
   - Category name.
   - Total amount and percentage of total expenses.
   - Whether the category share is "healthy", "high", or "excessive" for a typical budget.
   {{{end if}}}

4. **Cost reduction opportunities** — identify 3-5 specific expenses that can be reduced or eliminated. For each:
   - The expense name and current amount.
   - Recommended action (reduce, negotiate, eliminate, or switch provider).
   - Estimated monthly savings.
   - Effort level (low / medium / high).

5. **Savings optimization** — recommend how to allocate the freed-up cash flow across the user's financial goals. For each goal:
   - Suggested monthly allocation.
   - Estimated time to reach the goal.
   - A recommended account type or vehicle (e.g. high-yield savings, index fund, extra loan payment).

6. **Investment guidance** — given a [[[RISK_TOLERANCE]]] risk tolerance and the current savings gap, suggest a simple investment allocation (percentages across asset classes) for any surplus beyond the savings target. Explain the rationale in 2-3 sentences.

{{{if INCLUDE_QUICK_WINS}}}
7. **Quick wins** — list 3 actions that can be taken within 48 hours to improve the financial situation. Each:
   - The action.
   - Why it helps.
   - Estimated immediate impact.
{{{end if}}}

{{{if INCLUDE_90_DAY_PLAN}}}
8. **90-day action plan** — break the next 90 days into three 30-day phases. For each phase:
   - Focus area.
   - 2-3 concrete actions.
   - A measurable milestone to hit by the end of the phase.
{{{end if}}}

Conclude with a one-paragraph summary of the single most impactful change this person should make, given their goals and current cash flow.
